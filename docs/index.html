🚀 1-Click Web Installer

TimeStation MultiBand 6 Beacon

Flash your ESP32-C3 SuperMini directly from your browser.
Zero coding, zero terminal commands, and zero software installation.

⚠️ Your browser does not support WebSerial. Please open this page in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Brave.
⚠️ WebSerial API is not supported in this browser. Please use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on desktop.
1
Plug in your ESP32-C3 SuperMini: Use a certified USB-C data cable (avoid charge-only cables).
2
Enter Bootloader Mode: Press and hold `BOOT`, tap `RST`, then release `BOOT`.
3
Click Install: Click the button above, select your ESP32 port, and wait for flashing to reach 100%.
4
Configure Wi-Fi: Once flashed, ESP Web Tools allows configuring Wi-Fi directly, or connect to TimeStation-Setup-XXXX (192.168.4.1).
⚠️ Hardware Rule: Always connect your antenna circuit to GPIO 1. Never ground or connect the circuit to GPIO 2 during boot or flashing (it is an ESP32-C3 strapping pin).
